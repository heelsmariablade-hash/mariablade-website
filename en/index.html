Maria Blade

Dancer · teacher · author

“Movement as a way of knowing yourself.”

Dance practice, embodied awareness and an individual approach to movement.

Maria has taught dance since 2020, working with students of different ages and levels, in groups and one to one. Her work rests on classical dance technique, biomechanics, balance, coordination, musicality and expressiveness.

Her background in psychology gives her a sharper view of how body, attention and self-perception connect. Maria develops her own dance manner and visual identity, and takes part in dance camps, festivals and study trips across Europe.
